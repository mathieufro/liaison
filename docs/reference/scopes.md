description: "Every permission scope: the kernel's, and each shipped app's, with where the app declares it."

Generated by `bun run docs:gen` from `apps/api/src/modules/permissions/scope-catalog.ts` and the shipped app manifests. Do not edit.

Scopes are hierarchical capability strings, `app:resource:action`. Granting `app:resource` grants every action under it, and `app` grants the whole app. Roles grant scopes to members and agents.
